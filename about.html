About ILikeFish.Space

Last updated: 2026-10-24

What this site is

ILikeFish.Space is a free browser game arcade. It collects small HTML5 games in one place so you can start playing without an account, a download, or a storefront. The site is an independent project run by fishesarethings.

Everything here is designed to work in a modern browser on desktop, tablet, or phone. Many games also work offline once the site has been loaded.

What you can do here

  • Play instantly: open the games library and pick a game.
  • Play offline: the site is a progressive web app, so supported pages stay available without a connection.
  • Keep your progress: game progress and preferences are stored locally in your browser, not in an account.
  • Make your own: use the Game Maker to assemble a small game package and export it.

How it works

Games are plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript running in an iframe. There are no logins, purchases, or virtual currencies. Data such as settings and saved progress stays in your browser's local storage unless you clear it. Some games may load their own third-party libraries or services; see the Privacy Policy for details.

Who runs it

ILikeFish.Space is maintained by fishesarethings as a public project. The site is released under the Unlicense unless a game or asset says otherwise. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare.

How it is funded

The site is free to use. Advertising through Google AdSense helps cover the domain and hosting costs. You can control analytics and advertising choices in the cookie settings, and you can read more in the Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, game submissions, or removal requests are welcome. Visit the Contact page or open an issue on GitHub.
