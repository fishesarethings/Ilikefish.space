Rotaze

How to play Rotaze

Move with the arrow keys, WASD or the on-screen controls. The maze rotates as you travel, and the flag marks the exit. The minimap in the corner can be expanded to see the full layout.

Touch: Tap to play.

About this game

Rotaze takes the familiar maze and removes your sense of direction. Each step rotates the world, so the route you memorised is suddenly pointing somewhere else. Finding the flag means thinking in the maze’s frame of reference instead of your own.

It is genuinely challenging, and the game knows it: restarts are instant, levels are short, and the minimap in the corner shows the whole board so you always have something to reason from. There are classic, timed and dynamic-rotation modes plus coins to spend in the shop.

Tips and strategies

  • Use the minimap for planning and the main view for the next single step.
  • Expanded map mode pauses movement, which makes it a free thinking break.
  • If a route stops making sense, restart - short levels make it cheap.
  • In Dynamic Rotation mode the board spins on its own, so plan shorter moves.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the maze keep rotating?

Rotation is the core mechanic. Every move turns the world, so you have to reason about where things are relative to the maze rather than to the screen.

Are there different modes?

Yes - Classic, Timer Rush and Dynamic Rotation. Dynamic Rotation makes the board spin on its own, which is the hardest of the three.

What are coins for?

Coins collected while playing can be spent in the in-game shop on player colours, trails and skins.

Is Rotaze free to play?

Yes. Rotaze is completely free, needs no account or download, and there are no in-app purchases or loot boxes anywhere on ilikefish.space.

Can I play Rotaze offline?

Yes, once the site has loaded, the progressive web app caches the page so it keeps working without a connection - handy on planes, trains and patchy Wi-Fi.
