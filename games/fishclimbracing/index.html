How to play Fish Climb Racing — Swim Upstream

Tap or hold to swim forward, or use the on-screen controls. Keep your balance, ride the current when it helps and push hard on the straight sections.

Touch: Tap to play.

About this game

Fish Climb Racing is a physics game about momentum. Your fish is always being pushed back by the current, so progress comes from balancing thrust against control. Lean on the accelerator too hard and you flip; too timid and you drift backwards.

Runs are short and failure is funny rather than frustrating, which makes it easy to keep retrying for a slightly longer distance. Different sections of the river reward different approaches.

Tips and strategies

  • Feather the thrust through tight sections instead of holding it down.
  • Momentum carries you through gaps; jump before the current peaks, not during it.
  • Watching the surface is more useful than watching your fish when the water gets rough.

Frequently asked questions

Is it like a driving game?

It borrows the balance-and-momentum feel of climbing games, but you are swimming against a current instead of driving over hills.

How do I get a longer run?

Feather the thrust rather than holding it. Smooth corrections keep your fish stable, and stability is what gets you distance.
