How to play Fish Climb Racing

Swim up and down to dodge hazards and collect coins, and shoot to clear obstacles ahead. Distance is the score.

Coins unlock new fish in the shop. Each fish has its own shot and style, from bubbles and lasers to gold bars and electric zaps.

Keyboard: W/S or the arrow keys to swim, Space to shoot.
Touch: Drag to move, tap to shoot.

About this game

Fish Climb Racing is a side-scrolling swimmer with a shoot-em-up underneath. The current pushes you back, obstacles arrive faster the further you go, and each fish changes the feel: some fire bubbles, some fire lasers, some dash or shield.

The distance counter is the score, and the shop gives the run a longer-term goal beyond beating your last attempt.

Tips and strategies

  • Stay near the middle of the screen so you have room to dodge both ways.
  • Save the laser for clusters of obstacles rather than single ones.
  • Buy a fish whose shot matches how you like to play - speed, spread or power.
  • Watch the boss bar; it tells you when the next big obstacle is coming.

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock new fish?

Earn coins by swimming far and blasting obstacles, then spend them in the shop.

What does the laser do?

It fires a straight, fast shot that clears obstacles ahead. Different fish have different projectiles.

How is distance scored?

Every metre you survive adds to the counter. Hitting an obstacle ends the run.
