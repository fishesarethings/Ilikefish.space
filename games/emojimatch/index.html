Emoji Match

Two emojis, one phrase

How to play Emoji Match

Each level shows a phrase and a grid of emojis. Pick the emojis that complete the phrase; early levels need two, later ones need three or four.

Correct picks clear the level and move you on. Reset Level is there if you want to start the current puzzle again.

Mouse / touch: Tap or click the emojis.

About this game

Emoji Match is a small riddle game. The phrase is the clue and the emojis are the answer: for "Lion King", for example, you are looking for the crown and the lion. It is part vocabulary and part lateral thinking.

The difficulty comes from the number of emojis you need, not from a timer, so it works well in short sessions.

Tips and strategies

  • Read the phrase literally first, then think about the obvious symbols.
  • Look for pairs that form a well-known title or saying.
  • If a level feels impossible, reset it and start from the clearest emoji.

Frequently asked questions

How many emojis do I pick?

Two at the start, then three and four as the levels get harder. The instruction above the grid always tells you.

What if I get stuck?

Use Reset Level. There is no penalty for trying again.

Are the phrases always films?

No. The set mixes titles, sayings and common phrases.
