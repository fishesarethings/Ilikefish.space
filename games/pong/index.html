How to play Pong

Move your paddle with the arrow keys, the mouse, a gamepad stick or the on-screen arrows. Hit the ball back and score when it passes the other paddle.

Play solo against three difficulty levels, or two-player on the same device.

Keyboard: Arrow keys or W/S to move.
Mouse: Move the mouse to steer the paddle.
Gamepad: Left stick.
Touch: On-screen arrows.

About this game

This is Pong with a few modern conveniences: three bot difficulties, a two-player mode and a demo mode where two bots play behind the menu. The physics are deliberately simple, so angles matter more than speed.

The hard bot is beatable, but it punishes lazy returns.

Tips and strategies

  • Aim with the edge of the paddle to change the angle.
  • Return to the centre after every shot so you are ready for the next one.
  • Watch the demo mode to learn how the ball reacts to different paddle positions.
  • In two-player, aim away from your opponent rather than straight back.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play against a friend?

Yes. Two-player mode is local, on the same keyboard or with two gamepads.

How many difficulty levels?

Three: easy, medium and hard.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. There are on-screen arrows for touch devices.
