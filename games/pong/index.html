How to play Pong

Move your paddle with the arrow keys, the mouse, a gamepad stick or the on-screen arrows. Hit the ball back, and score when it slips past the other paddle.

Gamepad: Paddle Movement; Pause/Unpause

About this game

Pong is the game that started it all: two paddles, one ball and a very simple rule. Keep the ball in play, send it past your opponent and win the rally. Our version keeps that purity but adds a smooth, responsive paddle so your shots go exactly where you aim.

It is a great pick when you have two minutes to fill. Rallies start slow and build up, so early points are forgiving while long rallies turn into a genuine test of reaction time and nerve at the back of the court.

Tips and strategies

  • Aim with the edge of the paddle - hitting with the corner sends the ball off at a sharper angle.
  • Stay near the middle of your side so you can reach both corners in time.
  • Watch the ball, not your paddle; your hands will follow automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the original arcade Pong?

No. It is our own browser reworking of the bat-and-ball idea: same simple rules, new code, smoother paddle control and a version that runs in any modern browser.

Can two people play?

This version is single-player against the computer paddle. If you want local multiplayer, try Grid Bite or the two-player support in other games in the arcade.

Is Pong free to play?

Yes. Pong is completely free, needs no account or download, and there are no in-app purchases or loot boxes anywhere on ILikeFish.Space.

Can I play Pong offline?

Yes, once the site has loaded, the progressive web app caches the page so it keeps working without a connection - handy on planes, trains and patchy Wi-Fi.
