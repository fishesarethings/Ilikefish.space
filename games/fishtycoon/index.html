How to play Fish Tycoon

Tap or click to cast and catch fish. Sell your catch, then spend the money on upgrades that improve your gear and your income.

Touch: Tap buttons

About this game

Fish Tycoon is a fishing game with a business underneath it. Catching the fish is the fun part, but the progress comes from what you do with the money afterwards: better gear, better spots, bigger catches.

It sits comfortably between active play and idle growth. Early on you are reeling in almost every catch yourself; later, upgrades and automation do more of the work.

Tips and strategies

  • Reinvest early and often - a better rod pays for itself within a few catches.
  • Upgrade the boat or gear that increases income per second before cosmetic perks.
  • Leave the game running to collect passive catches while you are busy.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an active game or an idle one?

Both. Early on you catch fish by hand; later, gear and automation take over and the numbers grow while you are doing something else.

What should I upgrade first?

Anything that raises income per second. Better gear also makes individual catches worth more, so the two compound together.

Is Fish Tycoon free to play?

Yes. Fish Tycoon is completely free, needs no account or download, and there are no in-app purchases or loot boxes anywhere on ILikeFish.Space.

Can I play Fish Tycoon offline?

Yes, once the site has loaded, the progressive web app caches the page so it keeps working without a connection - handy on planes, trains and patchy Wi-Fi.
