How to play Why on Earth Is the Chicken Crossing the Road

Move with the arrow keys, WASD, a gamepad stick or the on-screen arrows. Dodge cars and the train, grab power-ups and swing the hammer when something is in your way.

Your score is how far you get. One hit ends the run, so the next attempt starts immediately.

Keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD to move, Space to swing the hammer.
Gamepad: Stick to move, action button to swing the hammer.
Touch: On-screen arrows.

About this game

This is a one-more-run road-crossing game. Traffic comes in lanes, the train sweeps across every tenth lane, and power-ups change the rules: boots make you faster, wings carry you forward and the hammer clears a path.

The joke is in the title, but the loop is genuinely tense: every lane is a small decision about whether to wait or commit.

Tips and strategies

  • Wait for a gap rather than sprinting into the first lane.
  • Do not stand on the train line; it comes back around.
  • Grab boots early - the extra speed makes later lanes easier.
  • Use the hammer before you are cornered, not after.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use the hammer?

Pick one up, then press the action button (Space or the gamepad button) to swing it.

What do the power-ups do?

Boots increase speed, wings carry you forward, and the hammer clears whatever is in front of you.

How is the score calculated?

It is based on how far up the road you get. A collision ends the run.
