How to play Why on Earth Is the Chicken Crossing the Road

Move with the arrow keys, WASD, a gamepad stick or the on-screen arrows. Press the action button to swing the hammer at whatever is in your way, and reach the far side of the road.

Gamepad: moveLeftRight; moveUpDown; useHammer
Touch: Tap the on-screen arrows to move the chicken.

About this game

Why on Earth Is the Chicken Crossing the Road answers its own question the only sensible way: by making you do the crossing. Traffic comes in waves, gaps are narrow, and the chicken has a hammer for reasons the game refuses to explain.

It is a light puzzle-platformer rather than a reflex test. Most levels are solved by watching the traffic pattern for a few seconds before you move rather than by sprinting straight across.

Tips and strategies

  • Study one wave of traffic before you step out - the gaps repeat predictably.
  • The hammer is for obstacles, not for traffic. Do not rely on it to survive a car.
  • Move up and down as well as sideways; the safest route is rarely the straight one.

Frequently asked questions

What is the hammer for?

It clears obstacles that block the road. It will not save you from traffic, so timing your crossing still matters more than swinging.

How long are the levels?

Levels are short enough to replay immediately after a mistake, which is the point: you learn the traffic pattern by trying again.
