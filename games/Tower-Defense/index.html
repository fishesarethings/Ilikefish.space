How to play Tower Defense

Click or tap a build spot to place a tower, then start the next wave. Towers fire automatically at enemies in range, and reaching the end of the path costs you lives.

Touch: Tap or click to play.

About this game

Tower Defense is the classic build-and-hold loop. Enemies walk a fixed path toward your base, you place towers beside it, and each wave gets harder than the last. Money spent in the wrong place early is money you do not have later.

The strategy is mostly about placement and timing. Towers that cover a corner get more shots per enemy than towers on a straight, and holding cash for one strong upgrade often beats spreading it thin.

Tips and strategies

  • Put your first towers on corners and bends where enemies stay in range longest.
  • Upgrade a few towers rather than building many weak ones.
  • Keep a reserve for the wave you cannot see yet; late waves punish overspending.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if enemies reach the end?

They cost you lives. When your lives run out the run ends, so leaks matter even in the early waves.

Should I build or upgrade first?

Early on, a few towers on corners beat many scattered ones. Upgrading a well-placed tower usually gives more damage per coin than adding another weak tower.
