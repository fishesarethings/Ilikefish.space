How to play Dice Roll Idle Empires 2

Tap or click to roll the dice. Coins earned per roll buy upgrades, and upgrades compound into much larger payouts over time.

Touch: Tap or click to play.

About this game

Dice Roll Idle Empires 2 keeps the original hook - every roll pays the number it lands on - and builds a longer progression around it. There are more upgrade layers, bigger milestones and a sharper sense of accelerating income.

If you enjoyed the first game, this is the same relaxing loop with more to buy and more numbers to watch climb.

Tips and strategies

  • Follow the milestone costs rather than the cheapest option; they unlock the biggest jumps.
  • Roll a few times before upgrading so you can afford the next tier in one purchase.
  • Leave the tab open when you step away so your empire keeps ticking over.

Do I need to play the first game?

No. The sequel stands on its own, though players of the original will recognise the roll-for-income loop immediately.

More upgrade layers, extra milestones and a faster sense of growth. The numbers climb further than the first game allowed.

Can I play Dice Roll Idle Empires 2 offline?
