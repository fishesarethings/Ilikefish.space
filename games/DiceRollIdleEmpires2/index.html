How to play Dice Roll Idle Empires 2

Roll the dice to earn money, then buy auto-rollers and upgrades in the shop. Prices climb faster than in the first game, so progress comes from stacking multipliers.

At $1e9 in lifetime earnings you can rebirth. Rebirth resets your money, auto-rollers and regular upgrades, but awards Lucky Shards and a permanent multiplier. Shards buy ascension upgrades that survive every rebirth.

Mouse / touch: Click or tap the dice, the shop buttons and the rebirth controls.

About this game

The sequel is deliberately slower and deeper than the first game. The early loop is the same - roll, buy, roll faster - but the economy is tuned so that a single upgrade line is not enough. You are expected to layer dice upgrades, auto-rollers, rebirth shards and permanent ascension bonuses.

Rebirth is the heart of it. The first one arrives late, and choosing when to reset is the main decision in the game.

Tips and strategies

  • Do not rebirth the moment it unlocks; a little more lifetime earnings means more shards.
  • Shard Gain is usually the best early ascension purchase because it compounds every future rebirth.
  • Switch to scientific notation once the numbers get long - it makes prices readable.
  • Ascension upgrades persist, so they are the safest long-term investment.

Frequently asked questions

What is rebirth?

A prestige reset. You lose money, auto-rollers and regular upgrades, but gain Lucky Shards and a permanent multiplier.

What are Lucky Shards?

The prestige currency. Spend them on ascension upgrades, which are not lost when you rebirth again.

Why is it called hard mode?

Prices grow faster and the milestone is much higher, so the game expects you to use prestige rather than a single long grind.
