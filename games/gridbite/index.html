Gridbite

Snake, but on a 16x16 grid with a boost button

Play in the arcade player Browse all games

How to play Gridbite

Steer your snake around the grid, eat apples to grow, and avoid the walls, your own tail and the other player.

Hold boost to move faster. In two-player mode, the last snake alive wins; in single-player, the score is the goal.

Keyboard: WASD for player one, arrow keys for player two, Shift to boost.
Gamepad: D-pad or left stick to move, face buttons to boost.
Touch: On-screen arrows, plus the boost button.

About this game

Gridbite is a local snake variant for one or two players. The 16x16 board is small enough that mistakes are permanent and boost is a genuine risk: it gets you to an apple first, but it also makes corners harder.

Two-player mode is the point. Cutting the other snake off is more satisfying than any high score.

Tips and strategies

  • Do not boost into a corner; the extra speed is hard to cancel.
  • In two-player, aim to cut off the other snake rather than racing for apples.
  • Stay near the centre so you always have an escape route.
  • Use the grid lines to judge distances before turning.

Frequently asked questions

Can two people play?

Yes. Two-player mode is local, on the same keyboard or with two gamepads.

What does boost do?

It doubles your speed while held. It is useful for reaching an apple first, but it makes tight turns harder.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. There are on-screen arrows and a boost button.
