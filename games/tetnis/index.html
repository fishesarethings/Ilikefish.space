How to play Tetnis

Use the arrow keys or on-screen controls to move and rotate the falling piece. Drop it into the stack, complete horizontal lines to clear them, and keep the pile below the top of the playfield.

Touch: Tap or swipe to move and rotate.

About this game

Tetnis is our tribute to the falling-block puzzle. Pieces drop, you rotate and slide them into place, and completed lines vanish to give you breathing room. The blocks are heavy, sharp-edged iron, which suits the slightly industrial look and the unforgiving pace.

What makes block puzzles endure is that the difficulty comes from your own earlier decisions. A greedy placement now becomes a problem two minutes later, so good runs are a mix of speed and discipline.

Tips and strategies

  • Leave one column free for the long piece instead of filling every gap immediately.
  • Clear four lines at once for the biggest score boost - it is worth holding a gap for.
  • Slow down when the stack gets high; panic placements end runs faster than speed does.

Frequently asked questions

What is different about Tetnis?

The pieces are drawn as sharp iron blocks and the pace ramps quickly, so the stack punishes greedy placements sooner than a slower block game would.

Is there a hold or next-piece preview?

The game uses the classic drop-and-place loop: rotate, slide, drop, clear lines. Plan around the gaps you leave rather than relying on extra tools.

Is Tetnis free to play?

Yes. Tetnis is completely free, needs no account or download, and there are no in-app purchases or loot boxes anywhere on ilikefish.space.

Can I play Tetnis offline?

Yes, once the site has loaded, the progressive web app caches the page so it keeps working without a connection - handy on planes, trains and patchy Wi-Fi.
