How to play Mine Tycoon

Click empty tunnel slots to build tunnels, then hire workers to mine ore into carts. The lift carries full carts to the surface for gold.

Upgrade worker speed, cart capacity and lift speed, and use rebirth when progress slows for a permanent multiplier.

Mouse / touch: Click tunnel slots and the shop buttons.

About this game

Mine Tycoon is a production-chain idle game. Ore is not money: workers fill carts, carts wait for the lift, and the lift turns ore into gold. Every upgrade removes one bottleneck, which immediately exposes the next.

Rebirth resets the mine but keeps a multiplier, so the second run is faster than the first.

Tips and strategies

  • Build tunnels before hiring; workers need somewhere to mine.
  • Upgrade cart capacity when carts fill faster than the lift can clear them.
  • Balance lift speed against worker speed so nothing waits.
  • Rebirth when upgrades start feeling slow - the multiplier is worth more than another level.

Frequently asked questions

How do workers earn money?

They mine ore into carts. The lift carries full carts to the surface, where the ore becomes gold.

What does the lift do?

It moves full carts from the tunnels to the surface. If it is too slow, ore backs up.

What is rebirth?

A reset that clears the mine but grants a permanent multiplier for the next run.
