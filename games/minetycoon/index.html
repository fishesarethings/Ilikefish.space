How to play ⛏️ Mine Tycoon ⛏️

Tap or click to dig and collect ore. Ore buys upgrades that increase how much you earn per dig and per second.

Touch: Tap to play.

About this game

Mine Tycoon is a mining idle game where every swing of the pickaxe is a small investment in a much bigger machine. You start with manual digging and finish with a self-running operation, upgrading equipment as ore piles up.

The decisions are about when to spend and when to push deeper. Deeper layers pay better but need better gear, so there is always a judgement call between saving up and pressing on.

Tips and strategies

  • Buy the upgrade that increases income per second before the one that only helps while you click.
  • Push to a new depth when a single dig is worth more than your cheapest upgrade.
  • Come back after a break - idle income keeps running.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to click constantly?

No. You can dig actively for a burst of income, but the upgrades you buy are what turn the game into a passive earner.

What is the best first purchase?

Something that increases income per second rather than per click. Passive income keeps working while you are not playing.
