How to play Lawn Mower Simulator

Drive with the arrow keys or WASD, or use the on-screen controls on a phone. Cut every patch of grass to earn coins, and use them to keep the mowing going.

Touch: Use arrow keys or WASD to move; mow grass for coins.

About this game

Lawn Mower Simulator is exactly what the name says, and that is the joke and the appeal. You drive a mower over a lawn, cut the grass, collect coins and watch an untidy patch of green turn into neat stripes.

There is no timer and nothing chasing you, which makes it a good game to play while listening to something else. The satisfaction comes from seeing an area go from messy to finished, one pass at a time.

Tips and strategies

  • Mow in long straight stripes - it is faster and looks better than wandering.
  • Check the edges of the map first; that is where uncut patches usually hide.
  • Coins come from coverage, not speed, so cover ground thoroughly.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a goal or a timer?

There is no timer and nothing chasing you. The goal is coverage: cut every patch of grass, collect the coins and enjoy a tidy lawn.

Why is a lawn mowing game relaxing?

Because nothing is at stake. There is no fail state, just a repetitive task that visibly improves the map as you work across it.

Is Lawn Mower Simulator free to play?

Yes. Lawn Mower Simulator is completely free, needs no account or download, and there are no in-app purchases or loot boxes anywhere on ilikefish.space.

Can I play Lawn Mower Simulator offline?
