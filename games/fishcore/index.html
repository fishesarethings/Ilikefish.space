How to play Fishcore: Data Dive

Swim up and dive with the mouse, Space/W/Up and S/Down. Collect data and avoid the corrupted fish-bots, piranhas, malware jellies and virus infections.

A boss appears every 1 GB of data. Credits earned in a run buy permanent upgrades such as speed, shield, armour and collection range.

Mouse: Click to swim up.
Keyboard: Space, W or Up to swim up; S or Down to dive.
Touch: On-screen controls.

About this game

Fishcore is an endless swimmer wrapped in a network-evolution joke. Each era changes the visuals and the hazards, and the upgrade tree lets you lean into speed, shields, armour, collection or hacking.

Bosses every gigabyte give the run a rhythm: collect, survive, fight, upgrade, repeat.

Tips and strategies

  • Collect data in safe lanes rather than diving through a crowd for one pickup.
  • Shield and armour are the safest early upgrades.
  • Learn the boss patterns - they repeat, and the gaps are generous once you see them.
  • Keep the combo window in mind; chaining pickups is worth more than rushing.

Frequently asked questions

How do I damage bosses?

Avoid their attacks and collect the power-ups that appear during the fight. The mission briefing explains the current boss.

What do credits buy?

Permanent upgrades between runs: speed, shield, battery, network, armour, turbo, collector and hacker.

What changes between eras?

The visual style and the mix of hazards. The core swim-and-collect loop stays the same.
