How to play Fishcore: Data Dive

Guide the fish with your finger, the mouse or the on-screen controls. Collect data pickups and avoid obstacles; the run ends when you lose control of the fish.

Touch: Move the fish with your finger.

About this game

Fishcore: Data Dive is a reactive arcade game about steering a fish through a world that keeps throwing more at you. Movement is direct - you guide the fish with your finger or mouse - so the skill is in reading the screen ahead and finding clean lines.

The difficulty curve is honest: the first stretch teaches you the patterns, then the pace picks up until the run ends. Sessions are short, and each one teaches you a slightly better route.

Tips and strategies

  • Look a third of the screen ahead instead of at the fish itself.
  • Make small, early corrections rather than waiting and swerving late.
  • Do not chase a risky pickup while the screen is crowded.

Frequently asked questions

How do I control the fish?

Direct movement: point, drag or use the on-screen controls. There is no button to attack, so all of your attention goes on steering.

Why do my runs end so quickly?

The pace increases with distance. Looking further ahead of the fish, instead of at it, is the single biggest improvement you can make.

Is Fishcore: Data Dive free to play?

Yes. Fishcore: Data Dive is completely free, needs no account or download, and there are no in-app purchases or loot boxes anywhere on ilikefish.space.

Can I play Fishcore: Data Dive offline?

Yes, once the site has loaded, the progressive web app caches the page so it keeps working without a connection - handy on planes, trains and patchy Wi-Fi.
