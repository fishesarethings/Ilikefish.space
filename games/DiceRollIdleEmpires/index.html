How to play Dice Roll Idle Empires

Click the dice to roll. Each roll pays out based on the number shown, and the money goes straight into your balance.

Open the dice shop to buy auto-rollers and dice upgrades. Auto-rollers earn for you between clicks; upgrades raise what each roll is worth.

Mouse / touch: Click or tap the dice to roll, and the shop buttons to buy upgrades.

About this game

Dice Roll Idle Empires is built around one loop: roll, earn, upgrade, roll faster. The first few minutes are manual, but auto-rollers and dice upgrades quickly turn it into a background game that ticks along while you do something else.

The pleasure is in watching the per-second range creep upward and deciding whether the next purchase should boost manual rolls or passive income.

Tips and strategies

  • Buy your first auto-roller early; passive income compounds while you are not clicking.
  • Keep manual click power and auto income roughly balanced.
  • Leave the tab open if you want the idle income to keep ticking.
  • Read the per-second range before buying - it tells you what a purchase actually adds.

Frequently asked questions

Does it keep earning when the tab is closed?

No. The idle income runs while the page is open. Closing the tab pauses it, but your saved progress stays.

What do dice upgrades do?

They raise the value of each roll, so both manual clicks and auto-rollers earn more.

Can I start over?

Yes. The reset button clears your progress after a confirmation step.
