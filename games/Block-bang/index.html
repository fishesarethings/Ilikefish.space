Block Bang

Drag blocks onto the grid and blow up full lines

Play in the arcade player Browse all games

How to play Block Bang

Drag one of the three shapes onto the 8x8 board. You score for every square you place, and a completed row or column explodes and clears itself.

The run ends when none of the remaining shapes fits anywhere. There is no timer, so you can plan each placement for as long as you like.

Mouse / touch: Drag a shape from the tray onto the board.

About this game

Block Bang is a fitting puzzle rather than a matching game. You get three shapes at a time and one small grid, and the challenge is deciding where each piece goes before the board runs out of room. Because rows and columns clear in both directions, one well-placed piece can set off several lines at once.

Runs are short and there is no timer, which makes it a good coffee-break game: you can think as long as you want, but the board does not forgive a careless placement.

Tips and strategies

  • Keep the middle of the board open; that is where awkward shapes fit.
  • Clear a row and a column together when you can - it is worth more than two separate lines.
  • Long pieces belong on the edges, not in the centre.
  • Look at all three shapes before placing the first one.

Frequently asked questions

How do I score points?

Every square you place scores. Clearing a row or column also removes those blocks and frees the space, which is how you keep a run going.

What happens when a shape does not fit?

The game ends. You always see three shapes at once, so keep an escape route for the largest one.

Is there a time limit?

No. Block Bang waits for you, which makes it easy to play in short bursts.
