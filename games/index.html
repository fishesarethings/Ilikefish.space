Free browser games you can play right now

ilikefish.space is a small arcade of eighteen hand-picked browser games. Everything here runs in the page - no installs, no accounts, no purchases and no waiting for a download. Puzzle fans can sink into Rotaze or Tetnis, idle players can build an empire in Fish Tycoon or Mine Tycoon, and anyone with two minutes to spare can jump into Gridbite or a quick round of Pong.

Games are grouped by mood rather than genre, because that is usually how people choose: something fast, something calm, something to think about. Every game page explains the controls, what the game is about and a few tips to get further, so you can tell in seconds whether it is for you.

The site is a progressive web app, so once a page has loaded it keeps working offline - useful on a train or a plane. Your progress and preferences stay in your own browser and are never uploaded.
