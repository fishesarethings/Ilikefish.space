Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how fishesarethings (the operator of ilikefish.space) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you visit or use the website. The site provides browser-based mini-games and related content. This site is largely static and stores most user-related game data locally in your browser (LocalStorage / IndexedDB).

Data We Collect

  • Local browser data: Game progress, preferences and other non-personal data stored locally in localStorage and IndexedDB.
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics).
  • Advertising: Data collected by Google AdSense (ad requests, cookies and measurement data).
  • Online game features: Some games use Google Firebase for anonymous sign-in and leaderboards; an anonymous user ID and game scores may be processed by Firebase.
  • Server logs: Standard logs by the hosting provider (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare).

How We Use Data

  • To provide and operate the site, including loading games and resources.
  • To store game progress and preferences locally.
  • To analyze site usage (aggregated, anonymized analytics).
  • To serve and manage ads (Google AdSense).
  • To provide optional online features such as leaderboards (Google Firebase).

Cookies & Local Storage

Cookies and local storage may be used for analytics, ads, and saving your progress. You can clear them through your browser settings. Export/import features are provided to let you manage your local game data directly.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics: aggregated usage statistics (https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Google AdSense: advertising (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
  • Google Firebase: anonymous sign-in, leaderboards and online game data (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

Advertising & Google AdSense

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites, and to measure ad performance. On pages where the cookie banner is shown, advertising cookies are loaded only if you allow them. Ads are labelled “Advertisement” and are placed outside the game area so they do not interfere with play.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.
