Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how fishesarethings (the operator of ilikefish.space) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you visit or use the website. The site provides browser-based mini-games and related content. This site is largely static and stores most user-related game data locally in your browser (LocalStorage / IndexedDB).

Data We Collect

  • Local browser data: Game progress, preferences and other non-personal data stored locally in localStorage and IndexedDB.
  • Recommendations: A local play history (games opened, time played, thumbs feedback and daily streak) is stored only in your browser to personalise game suggestions. It is not sent to us.
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics).
  • Advertising: Data collected by Google AdSense (ad requests, cookies and measurement data).
  • Online game features: Some games use Google Firebase for optional online features. If you use them they create an anonymous Firebase user ID and may store a display name you choose, scores, leaderboard entries, cloud saves, friend IDs and multiplayer room data. No email address, password or real name is required. A game only uses Firebase when you use one of those features, and the game itself shows when it does.
  • Server logs: Standard logs by the hosting provider (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare).

How We Use Data

  • To provide and operate the site, including loading games and resources.
  • To store game progress and preferences locally.
  • To analyze site usage (aggregated, anonymized analytics).
  • To serve and manage ads (Google AdSense).
  • To provide optional online features such as leaderboards (Google Firebase).

Cookies, Consent & Local Storage

Necessary storage (localStorage / IndexedDB) keeps the games and your progress working and is always active. Analytics and advertising storage are optional and only used after you allow them through the consent banner or the cookie settings you can reopen from any page footer.

We implement Google Consent Mode v2: all optional storage starts as denied, and only becomes granted for the categories you accept. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see Google's certified consent message (provided through Google AdSense), which manages the ad and measurement consent state for those regions.

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings. Export/import features are provided so you can manage your local game data directly.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics: aggregated usage statistics (https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Google AdSense: advertising (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
  • Google Firebase: anonymous sign-in, leaderboards and online game data (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

Advertising & Google AdSense

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites, and to measure ad performance. Outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, no ad request is made unless you allow the “Ads” category through the consent banner or cookie settings. Inside those regions Google's certified consent message collects your decision and Google determines which ad requests are permitted; where its policy allows, that can include limited or non-personalised ads when advertising consent is refused. Ads are labelled “Advertisement” and are placed outside the game area so they do not interfere with play.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Data Retention

  • Advertising: retention is controlled by Google's own policies for AdSense and the consent choices you make.
  • Server logs: kept by the hosting and CDN providers for a short period for security and troubleshooting.
