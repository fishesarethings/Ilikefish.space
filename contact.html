Get in touch

Contact is handled through GitHub. Please include the page URL and, for game issues, the game name and what happened.

  • GitHub: open an issue — the fastest way to reach us.
  • Repository: fishesarethings/Ilikefish.space

Replies usually take a few business days.

Copyright or removal requests

If you believe content on this site infringes your rights, contact us on GitHub with the game or page URL, the material concerned, and a short explanation of your rights. We will review the request and remove or disable the content where appropriate.

Privacy questions

For questions about analytics, advertising cookies, or local game data, see the Privacy Policy. To change your cookie choices, use cookie settings.

Before you send

Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information. Game progress is stored in your own browser, so we cannot recover it for you.
