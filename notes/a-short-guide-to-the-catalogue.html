A short guide to the catalogue

Notes · 4 November 2026

Eighteen games is small enough to browse and large enough that the first choice is not obvious. This is a short route through the catalogue by mood rather than by genre.

If you have five minutes

Why on Earth Is the Chicken Crossing the Road is the purest short-session game here: dodge traffic, grab a power-up, see how far you get, start again. Times Table Balloons is the same shape if you would rather do a little mental arithmetic.

If you want to think

Rotaze is the hardest puzzle in the catalogue. The maze rotates as you move, so the board you are looking at is not the board you are navigating. Tetnis and Block Bang are more traditional: one is a block stacker, the other a shape-fitting board. Emoji Match is the lightest of the puzzle group.

If you want to play with someone

Gridbite is the best two-player game here: a small snake arena with a boost button and a very short distance between confidence and a wall. Pong is the other local two-player option, and it has three bot difficulties if nobody else is around.

If you want something in the background

The idle games are built to be left open. Fish Tycoon is about building jetties and hiring fishermen; Mine Tycoon is a tunnel-and-cart production chain; Dice Roll Idle Empires is the simplest of the three. Its sequel, Dice Roll Idle Empires 2, adds a hard-mode economy and a prestige reset.

If you want action

Fishcore: Data Dive is a swimmer with bosses and an upgrade tree. Fish Climb Racing is a side-scrolling shooter with unlockable fish. Fish Battle I is a lane battle: spend Bubbles, defend the reef, sink the boat. Tower Defense is the strategy option, and Lawn Mower Simulator is the calm one.

If you are on a phone

Most games have touch controls. The ones that need a keyboard and mouse are hidden from the list on touch-only devices. If a game does appear, it has either on-screen controls or a control scheme that works with a thumb.

If you are not sure

Open the games page and use the search box. Every game page also lists its controls, a short guide and a few tips, so you can tell what you are getting into before you press play.
